Aswang

Aswangs have historically only been reported in the Philippines and surrounding areas but have been recently been increasingly common on a global scale. They are vampiric creatures empowered by death with seemingly no desire outside of attacking people.

Behavioral Modifier: Aswang's speed increases every time they kill. Walking through salt slows them.

Banshee

Banshees are characterized as harbingers of death and demise. They most commonly manifest as wailing and mournful spirits, occupying spaces where misfortune is bound to occur. Be less cautious of them, and more cautious of what they may be warning you of.

Behavioral Modifier: Banshees are more likely to break glass. They might use a distinct wail when hunting.

×

Demon

Use extreme caution when interacting with a demon! They are extremely hostile and dangerous!

Behavioral Modifier: Demons hunt frequently; crosses are more effective on them.

Dullahan

Dullahans, more commonly known as the Headless Horseman, are legendary characters of folklore. Often traveling long distances on their horses, they can be very dangerous when they decide to stick around.

Behavioral Modifier: Dullahans appear headless in photos. They move faster the longer they see their target.

×

Dybbuk

Dybbuks are malicious ghosts that have a strong bond with possession and pursue a specific goal or ideal before parting from the mortal plane. They have the unique ability of interacting with corpses.

Behavioral Modifier: Dybbuks can throw corpses. They are stunned when a Music Box is first played.

×

Entity

The entity is a powerful force that transcends typical hauntings. While its control over the environment is limited, it has been observed to teleport, granting it heavy influence.

Behavioral Modifier: The entity can teleport from one location to another. It is unlikely to throw objects.

Ghoul

Ghouls are regarded as troublemaking ghosts, more focused on "playing" than harming. They often engage in behavior to frighten people in their vicinity, but are quick to anger. Exercise caution when in the presence of a Ghoul.

Behavioral Modifier: Ghouls may become enraged if they hear talking. They can't disable electronics.

×

Keres

Keres are best described as angels of death. They wander aiming to feed on the souls of the deceased. They prey on the weakest targets that they are able to sense. It is best to remain mindful of your energy levels and keep them high when dealing with a Keres to avoid being targeted.

Behavioral Modifier: Keres' speed decreases with each kill. They target hunters with the lowest energy.

×

Leviathan

Leviathans are physical manifestations of chaos and destruction, and are as dangerous and unpredictable as you'd expect. They have a particular affinity for throwing objects.

Behavioral Modifier: Leviathans can throw multiple objects, and can disable lights passively.

Nightmare

Nightmares, as the name suggests, commonly manifest themselves as bad dreams and are usually harmless. However, particularly powerful nightmares can manifest themselves in the physical world, exhibiting much of the same behavior expected from an average ghost.

Behavioral Modifier: Nightmares can cause hallucinations. They hunt more often in the dark.

×

Oni

Onis are a class of ghost believed to originate in Japan, but sightings have been reported all across the globe. They are known for manifesting often and having a terrifying physical appearance.

Behavioral Modifier: Onis sprint during hunts. They manifest more commonly than other ghosts.

×

Phantom

Phantoms are ethereal apparitions that manifest less frequently than similar ghosts. They are misattributed as "shy" and "timid", but can certainly be aggressive. They are commonly associated with deep emotional attachments to specific locations.

Behavioral Modifier: Phantoms reduce sanity much quicker when looked at. Taking a photo of them will make them temporarily disappear.

Ravenger

Ravengers are stalker-class entities that slowly map out player pathfinding lines. They build aggression when left alone in corridors.

Behavioral Modifier: Gains tracking precision over time. Hiding completely resets its hunt speed acceleration.

×

Revenant

A violent, ancient spirit that hunts indiscriminately. It travels at terrifying speeds when it gains a line-of-sight visual on target items or players.

Behavioral Modifier: Highly accelerated speed when targeting open players. Travels incredibly slowly when players are hidden.

×

Shadow

shadows are barely perceptible apparitions that rarely interact with their environment, preferring to reside calmly in the dark. They are shy and tend to avoid people. though are known to be defensive of their favorite room.

Behavioral Modifier:Shadows do not alter the temperature much. They are less active in well-lit rooms.

Siren

Siren's are most known for their distinct singing voice which they commonly use to lure sailors to their demise, though they have also been seen roaming far away from the ocean. Keep an ear out for their distinct singing voice that you can hear through the spirit box.

Behavioral Modifier:>Sirens slow people they see while hunting. They always respond in a female voice.

×

Skinwalker

Skinwalkers are elusive ghosts with the unique ability to mimic the abilities of other ghost types. They are more frequent roamers. spending more time outside of their favorite room. Identifying Skinwalkers requires extreme precision.

Behavioral Modifier:Skinwalkers can fake ghost orb evidence. They can mimic other ghosts' abilities.

×

Specter

Specters, are strongly bound to their favorite room. When in the same room as one, many say they feel an ominous sense of unease. Specters are known for their profound control over the environment.

Behavioral Modifier:Specters are more likely to throw items. They do not roam except while hunting.

Spirit

Spirits are a manifestation of the souls of the deceased. They are able to project in the physical world and have been observed to manipulate electronic frequencies. They are often eager to communicate with the living and are generally considered harmless.

Behavioral Modifier:Spirits have no documented strengths. They can alter the flame color of candles.

×

Umbra

The namesake of the Umbra Board, Umbras are elusive, shadowy apparitions born from fear. Instead of planting their feet, they tip toe, making no sound as they walk. Their presence is often said to be deeply unsettling.

Behavioral Modifier:Umbras do not make footstep sounds. They move slower in lit rooms.

×

Vesper

Vespers are the malinant lingering souls of creatures living in complete darkness. they've evolved to rely solely on their auditory senses, a sort of echolocation, making them adept hunters despite lacking ocular vision of any kind.

Behavioral Modifier:Vespers rely solely on sound to hunt. They can track through walls.

Vex

A vex. while not a ghost in the traditional sense, is a presence that acts as the physical embodiment of anger and displeasure. They do not occupy the physical space in the same way we'd expect from other ghosts.

Behavioral Modifier:Vex's are invisible on LIDAR Scans. They can walk through walls.

×

Wendigo

Wendigos are horribly malformed creatures, less ghost and more monster caricature. Wendigos are incredibly afraid of fire, making candles and lanterns good tools for fending them off - still, it is best to be cautious.

Behavioral Modifier:Wendigos won't start a hunt near a lit flame. They move faster as average energy lowers.

×

The wisp

A will-o-the-wisp, commonly known as The Wisp or just "Wisp" is a spirit that typically haunts swamps, but have recently been observed invading homes and other buildings. They have a particular affinity for fire.

Behavioral Modifier:Wisps can light candles. They can only start a hunt in their favorite room.

Wraith

Wraiths are malevolent shadowy apparitions, noted for their profound aggression. They are known to inflict an impending sense of doom on people around them. Be wary when interacting with a wraith in any capacity.

Behavioral Modifier:Wraiths quickly deplete energy from hunters. They refuse to disturb lines of salt.
