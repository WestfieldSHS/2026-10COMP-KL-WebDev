Contract Modifiers

Adjust the risk parameters. Maximize your match payouts.

Player Settings

Starting Energy

0% - 100%

Controls how much stamina/sanity you begin the match with.

Type: Range Slider Risk: Variable

Energy Drink Restore

Modifier

Adjusts the energy percentage gained back from consuming energy items.

Type: Adjustment Risk: low

Energy Drain Rate

Multiplier

Multiplies how quickly your stamina decreases over time.

Type: Multiplier Risk: Medium

Player Walk Speed

Speed

Determines your movement speed across the contract map.

Type: Speed Control Risk: Variable

Flashlight Broken

Toggle

A toggle that makes your flashlight experience malfunctions or completely stop working.

Type: Binary Switch Risk: High

Max Stamina

Cap Limit

Caps your overall running and survival capacity during encounters.

Type: Threshold Cap Risk: Medium

Ghost & Hunt Settings

Can Ghosts Hunt

Master Toggle

A master toggle enabling or disabling entity hunting phases entirely.

Type: Core Switch Risk: Extreme

Hunt Duration

Time Limit

Dictates how long a single hunting phase lasts (e.g., 60 seconds).

Type: Duration Slider Risk: High

Hunt Energy

Penalty

The minimum threshold or drain penalty applied during active hunts.

Type: Drain Modifier Risk: low

Hunt Cooldown

Min 10s

Sets the mandatory down-time between consecutive hunts (can be lowered to 10 seconds for max difficulty).

Type: Cooldown Timer Risk: High

Grace Period

Min 0s

The time between the initial hunt audio/scream and when the ghost physically manifests to kill (can be set to 0 seconds).

Type: Buffer Timer Risk: Extreme

Hunt Probability

Chance %

The percentage likelihood that a random hunt triggers over time.

Type: RNG Percent Risk: Medium

Ghost Walk Speed

Up to 200%

Modifies the speed of the entity, adjustable up to a rapid 200% movement velocity.

Type: Speed Slider Risk: High

Kill at Hunt Time

Instant

Determines how quickly an entity can secure a kill during a phase.

Type: Time Threshold Risk: Extreme

Can Ghost Change Room

Toggle

Toggles whether the entity is locked to its favorite room or free to roam across the map.

Type: AI Roaming Risk: low

Interaction Rates

Multi-Slider

Individual sliders that control how frequently the ghost throws items (Action), causes jump scares (Interaction), and triggers events (Aggression).

Type: Activity Sliders Risk: Medium

Evidence & Match Investigation

Evidence Given

3 down to 0

Sets the number of evidence points the ghost leaves behind (3 for standard play down to 0 for the 4.07x challenge).

Type: Integer Count Risk: Extreme

Setup Duration

Timer

The initial "grace time" given at the start of a match before the ghost is allowed to interact or hunt.

Type: Safety Timer Risk: Low

Trace Duration

Up to 60s

Dictates how long standard physical traces (Handprints/Fingerprints) remain visible on doors and surfaces before fading.

Type: Fade Timer Risk: Medium

Monitor Broken

Toggle

A toggle option to corrupt or disable the data monitors located inside the base camp van.

Type: UI Blindness Risk: low

Map Settings

Cursed Possession

Toggle

Enables or disables the spawning of special items like the Music Box.

Type: Item Spawner Risk: Variable

Doors Open

Percentage

Configures the percentage of map doors that start fully open versus shut, modifying line-of-sight blocks.

Type: Map RNG State Risk: Medium

Fuse Box Starts On

Toggle

A toggle controlling whether the building power grid is active at spawn or requires finding the breaker room in total darkness.

Type: Power Switch Risk: High
